Features

Built for the ADHD brain

No overwhelming dashboards. No guilt trips. Just the right nudge at the right time.

🤖

AI Hype Buddy

Your personal AI coach that speaks ADHD fluently. Break any task into tiny steps, get gentle nudges, and talk by voice or text — anytime you need a boost.

✅

Habit Tracker

Track daily habits with beautiful streak visualizations. Each check-in earns XP and keeps your momentum alive. No shame if you miss a day — just start again.

⏱

Focus Timer

Pomodoro sessions designed for ADHD attention spans. Work in short bursts, earn bonus XP for completing sessions, and get automatic break reminders so you don't burn out.

🎁

Reward System

Earn XP for every habit and focus session, then unlock real rewards you set for yourself — coffee breaks, game time, movie nights. Dopamine hits that actually motivate.

Buddy Hype

Invite a friend and hype each other on. See their progress, send encouragement, and earn bonus XP for spreading the love. Accountability that doesn't feel like pressure.

Mood Check-ins

Tell the app your energy level and it adapts — low energy days get a simplified task list, high energy days get a challenge. Your hype buddy meets you where you are.
