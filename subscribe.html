Questions? We got you.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes! You can cancel your Pro subscription anytime from your Stripe billing portal. No questions asked, no hidden fees. Your Pro features will stay active until the end of your current billing period.
How does the 7-day free trial work?
You get full Pro access for 7 days — unlimited AI coaching, buddy renaming, custom rewards, everything. After 7 days, you'll be moved to the $9.99/month plan unless you cancel. You can cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged a thing.
What payment methods do you accept?
We use Stripe, which supports all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and many local payment methods depending on your region.
What's the difference between monthly and yearly?
Both plans include the exact same Pro features. The yearly plan saves you 15% — you pay $101.89 upfront (that's $8.49/month instead of $9.99). You also get exclusive yearly badges and priority support as a thank-you for committing to your growth.
Do I lose my data if I cancel Pro?
No — all your habits, streaks, XP, and history stay intact. If you've renamed your buddy, it'll revert to the default vibe name, but everything else stays exactly as it was.
Can I switch between monthly and yearly?
Yes! You can upgrade from monthly to yearly anytime through your Stripe billing portal — you'll get prorated credit for the remaining time on your monthly plan. To switch from yearly to monthly, just cancel your yearly plan and resubscribe monthly at the end of your billing period.
