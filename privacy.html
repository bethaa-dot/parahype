Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

The short version: We collect only what's needed to make ParaHype work for you. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. Your habits, tasks, and conversations with your buddy are yours.

Who we are

ParaHype is operated by Loading DrBetha Group LLC. This policy covers the ParaHype app at parahype.app and related services.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google — we never see your Google password.

When you use the app, we store your habits, tasks, task steps, daily completion logs, focus session records, XP totals, streak data, buddy vibe selection, and coach conversation history. This data is essential for the app to work.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data from other apps on your device.

How we use your data

Your data is used to provide and improve ParaHype's features, including habit tracking and streaks, task management, focus timer XP recording, AI coach conversations (sent to Anthropic's Claude API for responses), daily briefings and progress summaries, and reward unlocking.

AI coaching and your conversations

When you chat with your buddy, your messages are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate responses. We send your current habits, tasks, and XP context so the coach can give relevant advice. Anthropic processes these messages according to their own privacy policy. Coach conversation history is stored per-day and is not used to train AI models.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on Cloudflare's D1 database infrastructure. Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 (100,000 iterations) — we never store plain-text passwords. Authentication uses JWT tokens stored in your browser's localStorage.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only shared with Anthropic (for AI coach responses, limited to conversation context) and Cloudflare (infrastructure provider where your data is stored).

If you use the buddy invite feature, your name is included in the invite message you choose to send. We do not access your contacts.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to delete your account and all associated data, update your display name and email, and log out at any time (which clears your local session). To make a data request, contact us at the email below.

Cookies and local storage

ParaHype uses browser localStorage to store your authentication token, selected buddy voice, subscription status, and PWA install preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Children's privacy

ParaHype is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as ParaHype evolves. Significant changes will be communicated through the app. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

Contact

Questions or data requests? Reach us at: privacy@parahype.app
